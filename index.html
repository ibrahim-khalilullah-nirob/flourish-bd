Our story

Flourish BD started as a small page for handmade things we love making: flowers that do not wilt, pocket mirrors you can carry, and hair clips that feel personal.

Nothing here comes off a shelf. We make each piece after you order, so you can ask for your own colours, flowers and packaging.

How to order

No account, no checkout form. Just a message.

  1. Choose a product and tap Order on WhatsApp. The message fills in for you.
  2. Tell us your colours, quantity and delivery address.
  3. We confirm the price and delivery time, then start making it.
